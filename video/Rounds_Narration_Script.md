# Rounds: narration script (as spoken in `Rounds_Final_Demo_1080p.mp4`)

- **Voice:** Kokoro TTS v1.0 (open source, Apache-2.0, runs locally), voice `am_michael` (US English, male), speed 0.76. Generated by `voice.py`.
- **Why this voice:** 16 voices were compared by transcribing them with Whisper, clean and through a simulated laptop speaker (see `Rounds_Video_QA.md`). Kokoro's male US voices were understood perfectly; the previous macOS "Reed" voice was misheard most often.
- **Pace:** 255 words, about 110 words per minute while speaking. Pauses of 0.5–2.9 s fall between lines while the footage shows the action.
- **Placement:** every line starts at a fixed moment in its scene (when its action is on screen), or 0.5 s after the previous line.
- **Wording:** sentences were shortened and simplified for listening. Three words that the recognizer misheard were replaced: "reworded" → "paraphrased", "syncs" (heard as "sinks") was dropped, and the line starting "Rounds." → "With Rounds, …".
- **Text:** the subtitles (`Rounds_Subtitles.srt`) match this text word for word.
